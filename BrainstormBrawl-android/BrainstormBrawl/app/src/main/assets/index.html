How to play

Brawl: You and your rival answer the same survey question at the same time. Type as many answers as you can before time runs out.

Battle: Every correct answer makes your hero attack. Top answers hit harder. Drain your rival's HP to K.O. them, or have more HP left when time runs out.

Coins: 10 per correct answer, plus combo, star and boss bonuses. Spend them on heroes and powers.

Powers: Hint shows the first letter of an answer. +10 sec adds time. Freeze stops your rival for 8 seconds.

Bosses: Every 5th level is a villain. Beat them for +100 coins.
